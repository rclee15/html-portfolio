The Best Movies According to Rebecca

My top 3 movies of all time.

La La Land

I loved the visuals and music and it was a beautiful reminder of how our dreams in life are good, but not always realistic.

Big Hero 6

This is my favorite modern Disney movie and I feel like it does a great job of defining what it means to be a nerd.

Mulan

This is my favorite classic Disney movie and I always feel inspired because it reflects well on my culture.
